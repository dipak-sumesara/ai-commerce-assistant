# Shipping

Meridian Merch Co. supports standard ground shipping, expedited shipping, and freight coordination for bulk orders.

Shipping costs depend on destination, package weight, order size, and requested delivery speed. Exact shipping charges are confirmed at checkout or during quote review.

Orders may ship in multiple boxes or partial shipments when products come from different warehouse locations.

Customers can request blind shipping, event-address delivery, and split shipments for regional teams.
