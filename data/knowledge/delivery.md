# Delivery

Meridian Merch Co. delivers B2B merchandise orders within the continental United States. Standard catalog orders usually arrive in 5 to 8 business days after order approval.

Custom-branded orders require artwork approval before production begins. After artwork approval, most decorated apparel and drinkware orders arrive in 10 to 14 business days.

Rush delivery is available for selected in-stock products. Rush orders must be reviewed by the merchandising team before the order is accepted.

Large conference orders, split shipments, or orders with multiple decoration methods may take longer. The final delivery estimate is confirmed during order review.
