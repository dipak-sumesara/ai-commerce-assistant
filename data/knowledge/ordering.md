# Ordering

Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, colors, sizes, and decoration requirements. For branded merchandise, customers provide artwork files or request design assistance.

A quote is generated before custom production. The quote includes item pricing, decoration costs, estimated shipping, and expected production timing.

Custom orders require approval of a digital proof before production begins. Customers should carefully review product color, logo placement, spelling, and quantities before approving.

For large programs, customers can coordinate with the merchandising team for kitting, bulk shipping, or recurring replenishment.
