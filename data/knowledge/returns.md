# Returns

Undecorated stock merchandise can be returned within 30 days of delivery if items are unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.

Custom-branded merchandise cannot be returned unless the item arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from the approved proof.

Return requests must include the order number, affected product IDs, quantities, and photos when damage or decoration defects are reported.

Approved returns receive a replacement, account credit, or refund depending on the issue and the customer's purchasing agreement.
