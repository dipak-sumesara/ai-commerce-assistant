# Products

Meridian Merch Co. sells business merchandise for employee programs, conferences, onboarding kits, trade shows, and client gifts.

The catalog includes apparel, drinkware, bags, headwear, and outdoor merchandise from fictional listings inspired by common B2B merchandise categories.

Available brands in the demo catalog include Nike, Adidas, Carhartt, YETI, Stanley, and The North Face.

Product availability and inventory are based on the fictional local catalog used by this portfolio project. The assistant should not invent live inventory beyond the supplied catalog or knowledge context.
